About Prosenx

We built this because we've seen what happens when a permit expires without warning.

Prosenx was built for organisations where compliance isn't a formality - it's the difference between operating and shutting down. Oil and gas sites, engineering firms, environmental labs, logistics operators. Places where a missed renewal means a work stoppage, a fine, or worse.

We built the platform we wished existed: a structured, intelligent system that keeps every permit tracked, every document assigned, and every deadline visible before it becomes a problem.

What we believe

Our values

Operational clarity over startup complexity

The people using Prosenx are not looking for AI magic. They need to know which permits expire next month and who is responsible for renewing them. We build for that clarity, not for demos.

Accountability is designed in, not added on

Every document has an owner. Every action has a record. Prosenx is built around the assumption that when something goes wrong, you need to know exactly who owned it and what happened.

Your data stays yours

We do not sell data. We do not train models on your compliance documents. What you upload is encrypted, isolated to your organisation, and accessible only to your team.

Reliability before features

An alert that doesn't arrive is worse than no alert system at all. We invest in infrastructure reliability and delivery before we add new features. The basics must work every time.

Built for Nigeria, with the world in mind

We understand the regulatory environment of the Nigerian oil and gas sector, the NESREA requirements, the NUPRC permit categories. That context shapes everything we build.

Honest pricing

We charge a fair monthly fee and we tell you exactly what you get. No per-seat tricks, no usage limits hidden in fine print, no enterprise pricing that requires a procurement process.

The team

Who's building Prosenx

Prosenx is built by a small, focused team with one goal - making compliance tracking less painful for the organisations that depend on it most.

We don't have a large marketing team or a flashy headquarters. We have a product that works, and we're building it carefully - listening to the people who depend on it most.
