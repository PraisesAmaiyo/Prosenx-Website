Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

1. Who We Are

Prosenx is a compliance document and permit management platform operated by PBlaze Digital Limited ("Prosenx," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect from you, why we collect it, how we protect it, and what rights you have over it.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign up for Prosenx or use the platform, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name and organisation details
  • Business address (where provided)
  • Compliance documents, permits, and related files you upload
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, actions taken on the platform) for security and product improvement

We only collect what we need to run the platform, verify your account, and communicate with you about your subscription, document expiries, and account activity. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account and organisation
  • Store, organise, and track your compliance documents and their expiry dates
  • Send you account, billing, and document-expiry notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve platform reliability and security
  • Meet our own legal and regulatory obligations

4. How We Protect Your Data

Documents you upload are stored in encrypted cloud storage and are only accessible to authenticated users within your organisation, based on the role and permissions your organisation assigns them. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit and at rest, to protect your data.

That said, no cloud platform can guarantee absolute protection against every cybersecurity threat, including sophisticated attacks or compromises caused by exposed credentials on your side (for example, a shared or leaked password). We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to control who on your team has access to your Prosenx account.

5. Data Retention

We retain your documents and account data for as long as your account exists on Prosenx — whether you're on a paid plan or the Free plan, and even if you cancel a paid subscription. We don't automatically delete your data after cancellation or downgrade, since it remains yours to access.

If you'd like your account and all associated data permanently deleted, email us at info@prosenx.com and we'll process the request. A self-serve deletion option is on our roadmap.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you or your organisation are based, you may have rights under Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) or, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). These rights generally include the ability to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to our legal retention obligations
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain uses of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Who Can See Your Documents

Prosenx does not review, sell, or share the contents of your uploaded documents with third parties. Access is limited to authenticated members of your own organisation, based on their assigned role, and to Prosenx staff only where necessary to provide support or resolve a technical issue you've raised.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise your data rights, contact us at info@prosenx.com.
