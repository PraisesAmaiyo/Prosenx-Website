Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Prosenx collects user email addresses, company names, and uploaded compliance documentation to provide corporate tracking notifications. Uploaded documents are encrypted and accessible only by authenticated users within your account structure.

2. GDPR & Data Protection Rights

If you operate within the EU, you possess strict regulatory protections under the GDPR framework. This includes your rights to modify, object to, erase, or download a complete snapshot copy of any personal parameters or corporate data assets stored inside Prosenx structures.

3. Data Retention Policy

We retain document assets only as long as your corporate profile status is maintained active. Terminated accounts trigger systemic purging schedules across active storage databases within 30 days.
